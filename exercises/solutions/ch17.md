# Chapter 17: INSERT, UPDATE, and DELETE Without Destroying Data — Explained Solutions

For Level B, preview with `SELECT product_id, product_name, is_active ... WHERE is_active = false`, count the same rows, then update with the identical `WHERE` and `RETURNING`.

For Level C, ask whether completed orders should ever be rewritten to cancelled, whether the date condition is correct, whether the operation is meant for all branches, how many rows are expected, whether downstream accounting or delivery records depend on status, and whether a transaction and backup/restore plan are available. Technical syntax is not enough to approve a destructive business change.
