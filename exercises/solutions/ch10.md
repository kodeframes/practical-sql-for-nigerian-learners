# Chapter 10: Relationships, Primary Keys, and Foreign Keys — Explained Solutions

The foreign keys are `orders.branch_id`, `order_items.order_id`, `order_items.product_id`, and `payments.order_id` respectively.

`order_id` is a stable database identity; `order_number` is a business-facing identifier that also must not duplicate. Enforcing both protects different rules.

For split deliveries, remove the uniqueness rule on `deliveries.order_id`. The relationship becomes one order to many delivery rows. You may then need line-level shipment information if products can be split across deliveries.
