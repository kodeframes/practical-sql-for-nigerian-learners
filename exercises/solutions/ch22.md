# Chapter 22: Capstone: MarketLedger Business Analysis — Explained Solutions

An active customer might mean `customers.is_active = true`, which is an account-status rule, or “has at least one completed order in the last 90 days”, which is a behavioural rule. They answer different questions and can disagree.

A strong management note distinguishes account state, purchase behaviour, payment outcomes, delivery status, and stock movement from unsupported claims such as accounting profit or supplier reliability.
