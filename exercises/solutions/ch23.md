# Chapter 23: Capstone: School Fees and Logistics Problems — Explained Solutions

For school fees, a `fee_adjustments` table could represent waivers, credits, or approved adjustments so outstanding balance does not treat every difference as unpaid cash. One row could represent one authorised adjustment against an invoice.

For logistics, a route-distance or delivery-event table could add facts required for distance-normalised workload or more detailed lifecycle analysis. The correct addition depends on the metric you want to support.
