# Chapter 19: Database Design and Normalization — Explained Solutions

`student_payments` likely mixes student, programme, and payment facts. A better model could include `students`, `programmes`, an enrolment/invoice structure, and `payments`, depending on the actual rules. The key is to discover whether programme fees vary by term or student rather than blindly applying one template.

For the reporting request, measure the query, inspect the plan, check indexes and predicates, and decide whether a view or materialized summary solves the workload. Copying branch names into orders creates a maintenance rule whenever a branch name changes.
